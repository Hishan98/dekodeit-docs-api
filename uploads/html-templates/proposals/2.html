1. SCOPE OF SERVICES AND CHANGE MANAGEMENT

1.1. Scope Definition: The specific services to be performed, deliverables, milestones, payment schedule, and project timeline shall be fully and exclusively defined in a separate Statement of Work (SOW) document, which must be formally executed by both Parties. Each SOW, when executed, shall be incorporated into and governed by the terms of this MSA.

1.2. Strict Scope Limit (No Scope Creep): The Contractor is obligated to perform only those services and deliver only those Deliverables explicitly listed in the executed SOW. The Client acknowledges and agrees that any work, task, feature, or deliverable not expressly described in the SOW is strictly deemed Out-of-Scope.

1.3. Change Request Process: Should the Client request changes to the scope, deliverables, or timeline of an active SOW, the Client must submit a formal written Change Request. The Contractor will evaluate the request and provide a written Change Order detailing the impact on:

  1. Project Cost (additional fees).
  2. Project Timeline (schedule extension).
  3. Technical Specifications (feasibility and design).

No change shall be implemented until both Parties have formally executed the Change Order, at which point it becomes part of the governing SOW.

1.4. Exclusions: Unless explicitly and specifically included in an SOW, all services related to maintenance, support after the warranty period, external system integration (ERP, POS, accounting), data migration (beyond the initial agreed dataset), complex B2B logic, or international regulatory compliance are excluded.

2. PROJECT SCHEDULE AND CLIENT RESPONSIBILITIES

2.1. Project Timeline: The timeline specified in the SOW is an estimate and is contingent upon the timely fulfillment of all Client Obligations defined below.

2.2. Client Obligations (Pre-requisites): The Client shall be solely responsible for providing, in a timely manner and in a usable format, all items deemed "Pre-requisites" or "Client Obligations" in the SOW, which may include:

  • Formal SOW and Design Sign-offs.
  • Access credentials for all necessary third-party services (e.g., Payment Gateways, Hosting, SMS APIs).
  • Clean, accurate, and complete initial data (e.g., product lists, pricing, images, descriptions).
  • Prompt feedback and approvals on all deliverables within three (3) business days of submission.

2.3. Delay Due to Client Failure: The Client acknowledges that the Contractor's performance schedule is directly dependent on the timely completion of Client Obligations. The Contractor shall not be liable for any delays in the Project Timeline or inability to meet deadlines resulting from the Client's failure to provide any required Pre-requisite, feedback, or access on time. For every day the Client delays a required item, the Project Timeline shall be extended by one day, or more if required by the Contractor's resource allocation constraints.

3.1. Fees: The Client shall pay the Contractor the fees specified in the executed SOW. All fees are denominated in the currency specified in the SOW.

3.2. Milestone Payments: Payments shall be tied to the milestones and percentages defined in the SOW. The Contractor reserves the right to suspend work immediately if the Client fails to make any payment by the due date.

3.3. Taxes: The Client is responsible for paying all applicable taxes (including VAT, GST, or other sales taxes) related to the services provided, unless the Contractor is legally required to collect such taxes, in which case the Contractor will itemize them on the invoice.

3.4. Overdue Payments: Any payment not received within thirty (30) days of the due date shall be subject to a late interest fee of [Specify Interest Rate, e.g., 1.5%] per month, or the maximum amount permitted by law.

5. WARRANTY AND SUPPORT

5.1. Warranty Period: The Contractor warrants that for a period of Twelve (12) months following the Live Release or UAT sign-off date (the "Warranty Period"), the Deliverables will perform substantially in accordance with the specifications set forth in the SOW.

5.2. Remedial Action: If the Deliverables do not conform to the warranty during the Warranty Period, the Contractor will, as its sole obligation and the Client’s sole remedy, use commercially reasonable efforts to correct such non-conformance without additional charge.

5.3. Exclusions from Warranty: The warranty does not cover defects or non-conformities arising from:

  1. Use of the Deliverables in a manner inconsistent with the SOW or documentation.
  2. Modifications, alterations, or maintenance performed by anyone other than the Contractor.
  3. Client's failure to provide accurate data, timely access, or required third-party services.
  4. Failures or downtime originating from third-party hardware, software, or APIs (e.g., Payment Gateway, Hosting Provider).

6. TERMINATION AND DEFAULT

6.1. Termination for Convenience: The Client may terminate this MSA or any SOW for convenience at any time by providing thirty (30) days written notice. Upon such termination, the Client shall pay the Contractor for all services rendered and expenses incurred up to the date of termination.

6.2. Termination for Cause: Either Party may terminate this MSA or any SOW immediately upon written notice if the other Party materially breaches any provision of this MSA or an SOW and fails to cure such breach within thirty (30) days after receiving written notice.

6.3. Effect of Termination: Upon termination, the Receiving Party must immediately cease all use of the confidential information (Section 7) and return or destroy all copies. Termination of an SOW does not automatically terminate this MSA.
